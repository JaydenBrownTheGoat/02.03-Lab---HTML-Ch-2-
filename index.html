Middleton, USA

Location

Middleton is located about 8 miles Northeast going down I-84 from Caldwell in Idaho

About

Weather

Middleton is warm from around the start of Spring until around the middle of Fall and is very cold from then until the begining of Spring again, but just like the rest of Idaho you can get every season in one almost every day.

Population

There are about 9,425 people that live in Middleton as of 2020 but this small town has seen lots of growth in the last 6 years and is most likely closer to 12,800 people now.

Sports

Basketball and Football are really well known sports for Middleton, there are no crazy big popular teams that come from Middleton but there are a few high school teams for Basketball and Football respectively.

Things To Do

  • Hiking
  • Golfing
